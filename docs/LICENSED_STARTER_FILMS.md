Gran Dillama and Llamigos are directed by **Pablo Vazquez** according to their official credits. Llamigos' film asset also identifies producer Ton Roosendaal, music Torin Borrowdale and sound Sander Houtman. Preserve the complete film credits rather than presenting this as a complete crew list.

[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) permits commercial sharing/adaptation, subject to attribution, licence link, identifying modifications, and no additional legal or technological restrictions that remove the rights granted by the licence. Paid distribution does not create exclusive rights. Keep these licence rights clear to viewers; a free starter collection is a straightforward presentation. Do not suggest Blender endorses LingYing. Blender names/logos/trademarks are excluded from the general content licence under the [official Studio terms](https://studio.blender.org/terms-and-conditions/).

## Candidates deliberately not described as free downloads

- **Coffee Run**: [official licensing page](https://studio.blender.org/projects/coffee-run/pages/licensing/) explicitly permits commercial reuse under CC BY 4.0, and [the exact original film asset](https://studio.blender.org/projects/api/assets/5909/) is marked CC BY 4.0. However, that source currently says **Login to Download** and advertises Studio membership, so its original download was not verified as free for an anonymous visitor. Attribution: `(CC) Blender Foundation | studio.blender.org`.
- **Sprite Fright**: [exact original film asset](https://studio.blender.org/projects/api/assets/5454/) links CC BY 4.0, with full credits for complete screenings and `(CC) Blender Foundation | studio.blender.org` for excerpts; its source is also membership gated. Do not rely on the project's older About-page wording (which says a different licence version) when using the exact linked original asset.
- **Elephants Dream** has [official commercial reuse permissions](https://orange.blender.org/blog/creative-commons-license-2/) for the film under CC BY 2.5, but separately downloadable soundtrack audio is **CC BY-NC-ND 2.5**. Do not assume every asset from an open-movie project has the film's licence.
- A free-to-watch short on YouTube, TikTok, another drama app, or an archive is **not** by itself permission to download and republish. No modern Chinese short-drama catalogue was cleared in this research.

Choose content before importing; this report does not select or publish films on the owner's behalf.
