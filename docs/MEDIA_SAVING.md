# Media saving controls

LingYing discourages casual saving of its displayed covers and videos. Cover images disable native dragging and their context menu; supported browsers also suppress touch callouts. Viewer and private-preview videos request `controlsList="nodownload"`, suppress their context menu and native drag, and keep normal playback controls, seeking, fullscreen and picture-in-picture. Browser support varies.

The controls apply only to media. Visitors can still select and copy ordinary text, use forms and browser shortcuts, click posters and save a series to their library. Episode drag-and-drop ordering remains available in Studio.

These measures are **deterrents, not DRM or a guarantee against copying**. A browser must receive media to display it. Authorized viewers can retrieve those bytes, use browser tools, take screenshots or record playback. Public covers and free episodes remain public as required by the platform's browsing model. Paid episode streams continue to enforce current account entitlements, and Studio/admin previews retain their existing permission checks.

Cover and video watermarks are separate display overlays; they are not burned into stored files. Directly saved original media may therefore omit the logo, and browser-owned video fullscreen, casting or picture-in-picture may omit display overlays. No image/video files are rewritten and no duplicate media storage is added.

Stronger video protection would require a separate encrypted-streaming/DRM design, potentially using Widevine or FairPlay through a suitable provider, with its own integration and costs. Even DRM cannot guarantee prevention of all recording. Do not describe hiding Download or checking request headers as encryption or DRM.
