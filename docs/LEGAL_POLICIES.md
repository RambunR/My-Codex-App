## Accuracy and operational follow-through

The privacy policy describes the implemented account, identity-provider, media, payment, aggregate creator analytics, cookie, security/IP and coarse-country currency-display practices. A guest cookie's 30-day lifetime is not a promise that analytics records are deleted after 30 days. Hashed identifiers are pseudonymous. Cloudflare and other service providers may process technical information and process data outside Hong Kong. The policy does not claim absolute security, automatic account deletion, an advertising-consent mechanism, a fixed universal retention period or a geographical storage guarantee.

Access, correction, deletion/retention review, rights complaints and disputes are routed to the existing company contact, `contactus@hknovaxrproduction.com`. These are manual operational processes. The operator must monitor that inbox, verify identity proportionately, meet statutory response requirements, maintain and apply a retention schedule, protect processor arrangements, and investigate notices and appeals. Publishing text does not implement those processes or establish that the company already complies with every obligation.

The update is prospective and does not fabricate historical consent. Existing acceptance records keep their original versions, including `2026-11-08`; the storage increase does not retag old payments or require active creators to buy another Pass. New Pass checkout requests, including promotional and replacement checkouts, require `creator_consent` with `accepted: true` and both current versions. The API and checkout service reject missing/malformed acknowledgement or stale versions before provider work. The checkbox starts unchecked and resets on a new visit, account change or policy change; opening a policy link does not check it. Guests can register first, then must agree as the authenticated buyer before opening paid checkout.

The additive `creator_pass_consents` audit stores the authenticated account, payment and financial mode, policy/acknowledgement versions, selected interface language and server timestamp. A new payment and its acceptance are recorded atomically. Retries preserve the first record and the existing payment/idempotency key; SQL guards prevent overwriting the acceptance. An existing unpaid checkout can receive a new, explicitly supplied acknowledgement at the current time after ownership, quote and unpaid status checks. Existing paid transactions and late settlement, reconciliation or cancellation remain valid without inventing an earlier acceptance. Wallet funding does not use this consent.

The checkbox records an affirmative acknowledgement, not proof that the person actually read every word. It acknowledges the Privacy Policy rather than consenting to unrelated marketing. It does not create historical registration/OAuth consent records or require existing activated creators to buy another Pass. Material later amendments and separate marketing consent still need their appropriate notice, review and operational handling.

The policies do not promise immunity from suit or certify enforceability. The operator should have a Hong Kong lawyer review the actual business arrangements, consumer jurisdictions, copyright notice process, liability terms and translated versions. No business registration number, postal address or designated statutory copyright agent has been invented.
