# Hide and delete a creator's series

Creators manage their own series in [Creator Studio](https://lingyingdrama.com/studio), either from the series list or its editor. Administrator access continues to follow the existing server-side role checks.

## Hide and show again

Choose **Hide from public** on a published series and confirm. The series disappears from public discovery, details, covers and playback, including playback for people who previously unlocked episodes. Its episodes, likes, statistics, purchases and earnings are retained. The creator can still edit it, see its analytics and preview episodes privately in Studio without adding views or purchasing them.

Choose **Show to public** to publish the hidden series again. Existing unlocks work again, and its original first-publication date is preserved. Administrative takedowns and unresolved financial reviews still block creator actions; hiding does not bypass moderation.

## Permanent deletion

Choose **Delete series** to open a fresh impact summary. It shows:

- Episode, view and like counts, total and average viewing time, and stored media size.
- Distinct affected paid viewers across both live and test purchases, with separate live/test viewer and unlock counts.
- A warning that deletion cannot be recovered and does not automatically refund purchases or reverse earnings.

The creator must type the current title exactly and tick the irreversible-deletion acknowledgement before the final button is enabled. Cancel closes the warning without changing content. A server-issued, actor-bound confirmation expires after ten minutes. Changes to the series, its episodes or paid access require a refreshed summary and another confirmation. Ordinary incoming views and likes do not invalidate it.

Deletion removes the series from public, creator and administrator content views. It removes uploaded media and the series' content, playback history, viewing statistics, likes and saves. It cannot be republished, privately previewed or restored through moderation. Purchase records, financial ledgers and moderation/deletion audit records are retained; minimal inaccessible placeholders preserve financial references. No wallet credit, refund, revenue reversal or payout is generated by deletion. Financial disputes require separate administrative review.

## Media cleanup and storage

Public and private application access stops when deletion commits. Private uploaded R2 objects are removed through a persisted cleanup queue; storage quota is released only after removal succeeds. A temporary storage failure returns a successful deletion with cleanup pending, rather than reporting that the content remains recoverable. Cloudflare Durable Object alarms retry bounded batches; the standalone Node runtime also resumes pending cleanup on startup. Failed or late uploads are queued separately without releasing quota twice. Shared sample artwork and previews are not deleted.

Already buffered video or an unexpired signed URL issued by a standalone S3 deployment cannot be recalled instantly. Application deletion does not erase operator-managed backups or retained accounting/audit records; those follow the operator's retention policy.

## Validation

Automated tests use temporary local accounts, purchases and media. They cover authorization, hidden paid playback, republication dates, both financial partitions, expired/stale confirmations, irreversible deletion, retained financial records, exact quota release, cleanup retries and uploads racing deletion. Phone browser checks cover English, Traditional Chinese and RTL Arabic confirmations. The Cloudflare local runtime smoke exercises real R2/SQLite adapters and restart persistence. Release verification uses read-only production checks and does not hide or delete an existing production series.
